# About this project

## Why it exists

AI is changing who gets hired and what they're expected to know. Much of the free material online either lists topics without explaining them or exists to sell a course. This playbook aims to do the opposite: explain each idea plainly enough that a motivated beginner can understand it, build with it, and explain it to someone else.

## Principles

- **Understanding over links.** Every concept gets a short explanation, an analogy, an example and a self-check before any external source.
- **Accurate and dated.** Claims that can change carry sources and a `last_reviewed` date. Unverified stories are left out.
- **Original writing.** Notes are written for this playbook, not copied.
- **Free to learn, free to run.** No paywalls, no sign-ups, and every project's tests run without paid API keys.
- **No promotion.** Tools are named because you'll meet them at work. Nobody pays to be mentioned.

## Who's behind it

The playbook is an open-education initiative by [NeuralSaas AI](https://neuralsaasai.com), which builds secure AI systems for businesses. The NeuralSaas AI team maintains it as a public good, alongside contributors from the community. Content decisions follow [STYLE.md](STYLE.md) and are made on merit.
